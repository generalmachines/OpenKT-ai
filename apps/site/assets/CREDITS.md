# Image provenance

Every raster here carries its origin embedded in the file as well (JPEG COM);
`impeccable embed-prompt --scan apps/site/assets` verifies it.

All three engravings are public domain and were graded to one warm sepia so they read
as a single family. Cropping, desaturation and colour balance were applied with ffmpeg.

| File | Source |
|---|---|
| `hero-wall.jpg` | "Invention of printing: Gutenberg taking the first proof", 1869. Library of Congress Prints & Photographs Division, [LCCN 2006690328](https://lccn.loc.gov/2006690328). Public domain, via Wikimedia Commons. Cropped to 16:9 for the hero wallpaper. |
| `plate-compose.jpg` | "Impressio Librorum", plate 4 of *Nova Reperta*, drawn by Jan van der Straet (Stradanus), engraved by Philips Galle, circa 1600. The Metropolitan Museum of Art, CC0. Left crop: compositors at the type cases. |
| `plate-press.jpg` | Same engraving. Right crop: the pressman pulling the bar while the master reads. |
| `plate-proof.jpg` | Same engraving. Centre crop: a boy reading a freshly printed proof sheet. |
| `plate-study.jpg` | Albrecht Dürer, "Saint Jerome in His Study", 1514. National Gallery of Art, CC0, via Wikimedia Commons. Used for the access section. |
| `desktop-session.jpg` | Rendered from the approved design canvas (`design/canvas/Main.dc.html`) at 1280x800. Interface content is illustrative. |
